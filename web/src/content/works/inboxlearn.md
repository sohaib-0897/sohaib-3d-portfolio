Email and priority classification with a correction workflow and low-confidence review queue. Candidate models pass evaluation and promotion gates before release, with versioning and rollback for controlled updates.

## Demo evaluation

On a **fixed synthetic/demo dataset**, category accuracy improved from **50% → 90%**, and priority accuracy from **30% → 80%**. These results describe that demo evaluation, rather than general email performance.
